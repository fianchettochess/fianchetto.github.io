Everything you need

A complete chess workbench

From opening theory to endgame technique, Fianchetto covers every phase of your game with professional-grade tools.

📊

Interactive Evaluation Graph

Visualize the flow of every game with per-move tooltips, an evaluation bar, and engine recommendations rendered as board arrows.

🎯

Annotation-Driven Drills

Automatically generate drills from your own mistakes. Practice finding the better move at every inaccuracy, mistake, and blunder you played.

⚡

Tactics from Your Games

Personalized puzzles extracted from positions you've actually faced. Spaced repetition resurfaces weak spots and fades out the patterns you've mastered.

📖

Opening Repertoire

Build separate repertoires for White and Black with automatic gap analysis. Drill your lines with spaced repetition and export the whole tree as PGN.

♟️

Opening Explorer

ECO codes and named continuations built in, plus Lichess community and Masters databases. A personal opening book grows from your imported games with win/draw/loss stats.

📁

PGN & Bulk Import

Import 25,000+ games in the background without freezing the UI. Pulls directly from Chess.com and Lichess by username, with multi-encoding support for any PGN file.

⏱️

Play Any Way

Challenge Stockfish, Maia, or Reckless with time controls, play over-the-board with a built-in clock, or use the standalone chess clock. Experimental Square Off Pro and Chessnut board support over Bluetooth works on both platforms; turn-based online play via Game Center is Apple-only.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Your game library syncs across your Apple devices via your private CloudKit container — start analysis on Mac, pick it up on iPad or iPhone.

🏗️

Native Swift

Built entirely in Swift and SwiftUI — no web views, no Electron. Adaptive layouts for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with voice move input, keyboard shortcuts, and VoiceOver throughout. The Android port compiles the same native Swift core.

Triple Engine Analysis

Three engines, one goal

Fianchetto pairs the world's strongest chess engine with a neural network trained on human play — and a third engine to keep them honest. All three run on your device.

⚙ Stockfish

Unmatched Tactical Depth

The strongest open-source chess engine in the world, running locally. Find every tactic, refutation, and resource your opponents might find — before they do.

Depth: 1 – 40, configurable

Networks: Stockfish NNUE (~107 MB)
🧠 Maia

Human-Like Insight

Trained on millions of human games, Maia predicts what real players would play at your Elo level — not just the computer-optimal move. Learn natural, human chess rather than engine lines nobody plays.

Elo range: 600 – 2600

Model: Maia 3 CoreML (~44 MB)
🔥 Reckless
New in the latest beta

The Aggressive Second Opinion

An attacking engine tuned for sharp, sacrificial play. Reckless surfaces the bold, tactical lines Stockfish tends to play around — sacrifices and initiative where the opponent has only a handful of moves to survive — and cross-checks Stockfish so you see exactly where the two disagree: the moments most worth a closer look. Also available as a sparring partner.

Role: Sharp lines, second opinion & play

Network: Reckless NNUE (~5 MB)

Privacy First

Your games stay yours

Platforms

macOS, iPadOS, iOS — and Android on the way

Fianchetto is built as a native app for Apple platforms, with an Android version under active development and at broad feature parity — the three engines, consensus annotation, repertoire, tactics, endgame, opening explorer, stats, voice move input, and both Square Off Pro and Chessnut boards all work on Android too. A few things stay Apple-only today: iCloud sync (Android moves data via file backups instead), Game Center online play, repertoire PDF export, and the macOS auxiliary windows / iPad multitasking layouts.

The app is currently in pre-release development (version 0.10.x) and is not yet available on the App Store or Google Play.
