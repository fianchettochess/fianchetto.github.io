§I
The Engines

All three run entirely on your device. No server. No subscription.

Three engines, one goal.

Three independent analytical minds — each seeing the same position differently. Stockfish, Maia, Reckless
Stockfish

Unmatched Tactical Depth

The strongest open-source chess engine in the world, running locally. Find every tactic, refutation, and resource your opponents might find — before they do.

  • Depth: 1 – 40, configurable
  • Network: Stockfish NNUE (~107 MB)
  • Role: Primary analysis engine
Maia

Human-Like Insight

Trained on millions of human games, Maia predicts what real players would play at your Elo level — not just the computer-optimal move. Learn natural, human chess rather than engine lines nobody plays.

  • Elo range: 600 – 2600
  • Model: Maia 3 CoreML (~44 MB)
  • Role: Human-style prediction & play
RecklessNew in beta

The Aggressive Second Opinion

An attacking engine tuned for sharp, sacrificial play. Reckless surfaces the bold, tactical lines Stockfish tends to play around — and cross-checks Stockfish so you see exactly where the two disagree: the moments most worth a closer look.

  • Network: Reckless NNUE (~60 MB)
  • Role: Sharp lines, second opinion & play
§II
Features

From opening theory to endgame technique, Fianchetto covers every phase of your game.

A complete chess workbench.

Fianchetto is built for the player who wants to understand the game — not just play it. Every feature is designed around the same premise: what does a serious student actually need?

01

Interactive Evaluation Graph

Visualize the flow of every game with per-move tooltips, an evaluation bar, and engine recommendations rendered as board arrows.

02

Annotation-Driven Drills

Automatically generate drills from your own mistakes. Practice finding the better move at every inaccuracy, mistake, and blunder you played.

03

Tactics from Your Games

Personalized puzzles extracted from positions you've actually faced. Spaced repetition resurfaces weak spots and fades out patterns you've mastered.

04

Opening Repertoire

Build separate repertoires for White and Black with automatic gap analysis. Drill your lines with spaced repetition and export the whole tree as PGN.

05

Opening Explorer

ECO codes and named continuations built in, plus Lichess community and Masters databases. A personal opening book grows from your imported games with win/draw/loss stats.

06

PGN & Bulk Import

Import 25,000+ games in the background without freezing the UI. Pulls directly from Chess.com and Lichess by username, with multi-encoding support for any PGN file.

07

Play Any Way

Challenge Stockfish, Maia, or Reckless with time controls, play over-the-board with a built-in clock, or use the standalone chess clock. Square Off Pro, Chessnut, ChessUp 2, and Millennium board support over Bluetooth.

08

iCloud Sync

Your game library syncs across your Apple devices via your private CloudKit container — start analysis on Mac, pick it up on iPad or iPhone.

09

Native Swift

Built entirely in Swift and SwiftUI — no web views, no Electron. Adaptive layouts for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with voice move input, keyboard shortcuts, and VoiceOver throughout. The Android port compiles the same native Swift core.

Bring your history with you.

Import your entire game library from any source. 25,000+ games in the background, without freezing the UI. Multi-encoding support for any archive you carry.

Source I
Chess.com

Import all your games directly by username — no export required. Your full history, without ceremony.

Source II
Lichess

Pull your full game history by username, or share a Lichess game link straight into the app. Free APIs power opening explorer and tablebase too.

Source III
PGN Files

Drop in any PGN file. Supports UTF-8, Latin-1, and Windows-1252 encodings — whatever archive you carry.

§IV
Privacy

Fianchetto is built offline-first. Stockfish, Maia, and Reckless run entirely on your device — no server, no account, no subscription.

  • — All engine analysis runs on-device; the engines never phone home
  • — No account required — no sign-up, no login, no servers of ours
  • — No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking — nothing is collected
  • — Optional iCloud sync uses your own private CloudKit container
  • — Chess.com, Lichess, and tablebase requests happen only when you use those features
  • — Free software under GPL-3.0 — source code published when the app ships
All analysis is local.

Android
In development

Fianchetto is built as a native app for Apple platforms, with an Android version under active development at broad feature parity — the three engines, consensus annotation, repertoire, tactics, endgame, opening explorer, stats, voice move input, and the Square Off Pro, Chessnut, ChessUp 2, and Millennium boards all work on Android too.

A few things stay Apple-only today: iCloud sync (Android uses file backups instead), Game Center online play, repertoire PDF export, and the macOS auxiliary windows / iPad multitasking layouts.

The app is currently in pre-release development (version 0.11.x) and is not yet available on the App Store or Google Play.
