Fianchetto

A native chess workbench for studying, training, and improving.

Analyze your games with world-class engines, drill tactics mined from your own mistakes, and build an opening repertoire that actually sticks — all on-device, no account required.

What it does

One workbench for your whole chess life

  • Three engines, all on-device

    Stockfish 18 for objective analysis with adjustable depth (1–40) and multiple lines. Maia 3, a neural network trained on human games, for human-like play and analysis at any rating from 600 to 2600. Reckless (new in the latest beta) as a second-opinion engine that cross-checks Stockfish and highlights where they disagree.

    Every engine runs entirely on your device — your analysis stays local unless you turn on the online opening explorer or tablebase lookups.

  • Game review & accuracy

    One-tap annotation classifies every move from brilliant to blunder, with per-side accuracy, auto-play replay, and critical-moment highlights. Quiz mode turns every flagged mistake into a multiple-choice drill, and Guess Elo estimates the strength of play. Track accuracy by color, phase, and time control across your whole library.

  • Training built from your games

    Tactics puzzles generated from your own annotated games, curated practice studies (checkmate patterns, forks, pins, pawn endgames), trap drills, and an endgame trainer with classic archetypes — Lucena, Philidor, K+B+N, and more — verified against tablebases, with an offline Stockfish fallback.

  • Repertoire that sticks

    Build and drill openings in one place: anchored sub-repertoires, gap detection, a Stockfish audit that flags unsound lines, and spaced repetition weighted toward the moves you find counterintuitive. A Punish mode drills refutations of your opponents' natural-but-unsound replies. Import and export as PGN.

  • Library, import & sync

    Import PGN files, share Lichess links straight into the app, or pull your games from Chess.com and Lichess by username — no login required. Full backup and restore to a documented open JSON format. On Apple platforms, your library syncs privately across devices via iCloud.

  • Play, your way

    Play against Stockfish or Maia, pass-and-play over the board on one device, or use the standalone chess clock with bullet, blitz, rapid, and classical presets. Turn-based online play via Game Center and experimental support for the Square Off Pro electronic board over Bluetooth are in the works on Apple platforms.

  • Made to feel native

    Three piece sets, six board themes, a FEN board editor, copy-position-as-image, keyboard shortcuts, VoiceOver support, voice move entry on iOS, and multi-board sessions on iPad and Mac. No web views pretending to be an app.

  • Private by design

    No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Analysis stays on your device; the only network requests are the ones you ask for, like importing your games. Read the full privacy policy.

Platforms

macOS, iPadOS, iOS — and Android on the way

Fianchetto is built as a native app for Apple platforms, with an Android version under active development and nearing feature parity. A few features are Apple-only today: Game Center online play, Square Off board support, voice move entry, and multi-board sessions.

The app is currently in pre-release development (version 0.9.x) and is not yet available on the App Store or Google Play.
