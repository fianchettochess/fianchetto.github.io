Fianchetto includes Stockfish, the UCI chess engine by the Stockfish developers, derived from Glaurung 2.1 (GPL-3.0); Maia 3, the human-like neural network chess engine by CSSLab, University of Toronto (AGPL-3.0, used with permission of the authors); and Reckless, the UCI chess engine by Arseniy Surkov (AGPL-3.0). Cburnett piece graphics by Colin M. L. Burnett and Merida piece graphics by Armando Hernandez Marroquin (both GPL-2.0+), plus the opening book (CC0), come via Lichess, whose free APIs also power opening explorer, tablebase, and game import features. The board diagram on the home page uses the app's bundled Inkwell piece set.
